# Dialogue notes (unverified culture details for the owner to check)
Text lives in localization/dialogue.csv; lines in content/dialogue/dialogues.json.
- "jambiyah": spelling follows the project's own docs.
- "qishr": a Yemeni drink brewed from coffee husks; Bilqis jokes that the jambiyah was a story told over it.
- "Jiddi": my grandfather (Arabic, dialect form). Zayd uses it for the grandfather who gave him the blade.
- The Concession patrol and drones are this game's own setting (docs/design/world.md), not real places.
