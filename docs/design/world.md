# World, cast and the jambiyah (draft for owner review; unverified culture details: owner corrects)
## World
Sana'a in 2150: the Old City's tower-houses (stained-glass qamariyya windows, white gypsum trim) are the core
of a neon megacity. Water is the scarce power: the Concession (a corporation) rations it. Mood: warm
brick, indigo night, neon in Musnad letters, drones among minarets. Chapter 1 slice: Old City souq and rooftops.
## Cast (invented; names are placeholders)
- Zayd al-Sana'ani, 28: hero, a courier-fixer of the souq guild. Carries his grandfather's jambiyah.
- Bilqis, 26: hacker-engineer, runs a coffee stall as cover; drones and debuffs.
- Sheikh Hamoud, 58: ex-guard of the Old City, elder; shield, taunt, heavy hits.
- Director Idris Qadi: the Concession's water director; Chapter 1 antagonist.
## The jambiyah (hero weapon)
Two slots, changed separately at a workshop, each with its own upgrade track:
- Hilt: sets the style: timeline speed (charge time), stance, the active ability, element.
- Blade: sets the damage: type, reach, interrupt power, the passive.
Combos come from pairing (a light hilt with a heavy blade = fast hits that stagger). Rules to define in
combat.md with tests: stat merge order, one ability per hilt, upgrade levels, no pairing that breaks timeline caps.
