# Chapter 1 slice: "The Water Ledger" (~30 min; draft for owner review)
Setting and cast: world.md. Rules: combat.md. Prototype v0 covers beats 3-5 (one fight). (check) = unsure culture detail.
## Pitch
Dawn in the Old City, 2150. The Concession cuts the souq's water ration again. Zayd, a courier-fixer, is paid
to carry a sealed ledger across the rooftops; Bilqis sees the seal is the Concession's own. Sheikh Hamoud,
who knew Zayd's grandfather, helps him fit the family djanbeyyah. They follow the ledger into the old cistern,
then to the pumping station, where Director Idris Qadi shows the shortage is a choice.
## Beats (minutes are a target for a first-time player)
| # | Min | Area | Player does | Story beat | Teaches |
|---|-----|------|-------------|------------|---------|
| 1 | 0-3 | Souq | Walk, take the ledger job | Zayd's morning, the water queue | Walking, interact key |
| 2 | 3-6 | Souq | Talk to Bilqis and two stall folk | She reads the seal: Concession | Dialogue, choices, party |
| 3 | 6-10 | Rooftops | Cross parapets, get caught | Enforcers want the ledger | Timeline battle 1, wait mode |
| 4 | 10-13 | Workshop | Meet Hamoud, set hilt and blade | Grandfather's djanbeyyah | Two-part weapon menu |
| 5 | 13-17 | Rooftops | Fight a drone patrol | Hamoud joins the party | Interrupts: cancel a charge |
| 6 | 17-21 | Cistern | Solve the sluice puzzle | Old water map; Bilqis hacks | One puzzle, her skill |
| 7 | 21-24 | Cistern | Fight under the arches | Hamoud shields the party | Rows, cover, taunt |
| 8 | 24-28 | Station | Idris talks, then the boss | The shortage is on purpose | Boss with a big charge |
| 9 | 28-30 | Station | Short scene, walk out | The hook (below) | Reward: a new blade part |
## Areas (5)
- Old City souq: tower-houses, awnings, neon Arabic signs, water queue; safe, talkative, warm brick.
- Rooftops: parapets, laundry lines, drones between minarets; enforcer patrols; open view.
- Hamoud's workshop: a tower-house room with a bench and qamariyya light; the weapon menu lives here.
- Old cistern: dark stone arches, sluice gates, cool blue light, dripping; the puzzle and one fight.
- Concession pumping station: clean steel, white light, cold against the Old City; the boss.
## Encounters (4 fights; HP and SP refill after each; losing offers an instant retry)
- Rooftops, beat 3: 2 enforcers. Wait mode, pick a move and a target. Hard to lose.
- Rooftops, beat 5: 1 enforcer, 1 survey drone. The drone charges a scan: cancel it with a quick move.
- Cistern, beat 7: 2 enforcers in front, 1 drone behind. Cover and rows: break the front to reach the drone.
- Station, beat 8: Idris + 2 enforcers. Idris charges a heavy strike every few turns; interrupt it or Hamoud
  takes the hit. Calm, precise, never raises his voice. Winning drops a blade part.
## Puzzle (beat 6)
Two sluice gates and a floating weight: open them in the right order to lift the lock grate. Bilqis's drone
scans the wall map and hints on request. A wrong order only drains the pool back: no timer, no fail state.
## Ending hook
Idris falls but does not run. He says the ledger lists every water source in Sana'a, and that the cistern was
sealed with a mark Zayd knows: his grandfather's djanbeyyah engraving (check). A locked door opens to it.
Chapter 2 starts below the city.
## Respect notes
- Sana'a is shown living and proud: craft, hospitality, humour, family. The enemy is a corporation, not a people.
- Coffee at Bilqis's stall: Yemeni coffee culture is real; serving details (husk coffee, ginger) (check).
- How a stranger greets and addresses an elder like Hamoud (check). The djanbeyyah is worn on a belt, a
  cultural object more than a weapon (check). Arabic lines get a native review later.
