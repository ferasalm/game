# Owner playtest, 2026-10-23: findings and plan
Feras played v0.3 (6 screenshots in chat). Each item: what he saw, the cause or plan, the step that owns it.

## Bugs (one small fix chat, Sonnet medium; or one agy brief)
1. Footsteps loud, harsh and out of sync. Plan: trigger steps from foot contact (locomotion phase / clip events),
   not a timer; lower the volume in content; softer samples (CC0) if the current ones stay harsh.
2. Abu Nabil stands inside his stall (screenshot 1). Plan: his stand spot in front of or behind the counter, from
   the stall's own footprint, plus a test that no NPC overlaps a prop's collision.
3. Chests look open and closed at once (screenshot 2): the open lid floats above the closed box. Plan: one lid
   mesh, closed until opened, then rotated open; test the state per chest.
4. Zayd's tech moves all read "[cancels]" and hit softer than Jambiyah Strike (screenshot 6). Cause: the kit in
   docs/design/zayd-moves.md (one job per move) is designed but not built. Hold it until the combat-style question
   (below) is settled; a content-only stopgap can give each move its job's numbers.

## Level and environment (dedicated effort: Level Designer, after the look and camera are chosen)
5. The roof is too big and lost its second roof. Feras wanted v1's two connected roofs, just less crowded.
   docs/design/rooftop-v3.md (two terraces joined by a bridge) is that layout; rebuild from it, not from v2.
6. Buildings dither away up close but can't be entered. Feras wants a Sana'ani tayramana (rooftop room with large
   windows) holding a Yemeni majlis (maqyal, low cushions along the walls) that the party enters, sits in, and
   shares qishr with Bilqis (a talk scene). New: an interior scene, a door transition, a sit interaction, a dialogue.
7. The workshop is plain (screenshot 4), unlike the designed one (art-bible: low table, glowing screen, tools, a
   brass-hilted blade; a WIP bench prop sits unmerged in worktree-agent-a4cf40c0e9518e744). Plan: finish that prop
   with the dressing pass.

## Direction questions (Feras decides; nothing built before)
